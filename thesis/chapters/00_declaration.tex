\vspace{1.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Statement on the use of generative AI tools.}
In the course of preparing this thesis I used AI-based assistance tools
(in particular Anthropic's Claude) for the following purposes:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Language editing of text I had written myself (grammar, phrasing and clarity).
  \item Refactoring and documentation of the analysis code and assistance with debugging. That includes reorganising and de-duplicating the R scripts. I verified that any results affected by this remained unchanged.
  \item Generation of code to visualize results in tables and plots.
\end{itemize}
\noindent The research question, the study design, the data processing, the
choice and implementation of the econometric methods, the empirical results and
their interpretation, and all conclusions are my own work. AI tools were not
used to generate research findings, data, or references, and all AI-assisted
output was reviewed and verified by me. I take full responsibility for the
entire content of this thesis.
