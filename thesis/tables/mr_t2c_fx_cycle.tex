\tabnotes{For each country, the twelve-month currency-return component of the
  US-dollar excess return---$\rx^{\mathrm{USD}}_{i,t+12}-\rx_{i,t+12}$ in the
  duration-standardised units of \eqref{eq:rx-usd}---is correlated with the
  local cycle factor $\CF_{i,t}$ over the country's estimation sample. The last
  column reports the standard deviation of that currency component. The US
  carries no currency leg, so its currency return is identically zero. The
  currency return is at most weakly related to the bond cycle in every market
  (absolute correlation never exceeds $0.34$), so currency conversion adds
  largely independent noise rather than being absorbed by the bond factor.}
