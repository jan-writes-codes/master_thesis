\tabnotes{The global cycle factor $\GCF_{t}$ prices the hedged local-currency
  premium; the FX-adjusted factor $\FXGCF_{t}$ prices the unhedged US-dollar
  premium; their difference (the ``wedge'') isolates the currency component,
  $\FXGCF_{t}-\GCF_{t}=\sum_{i}w_{i,t}(\FXCF_{i,t}-\CF_{i,t})$.
  Both factors are GDP-weighted aggregates of the per-country USD/local cycle
  factors under the bottom-up construction \eqref{eq:fxgcf}. AR(1) is the
  monthly first-order autocorrelation and the half-life is
  $\ln(0.5)/\ln(\mathrm{AR}(1))$. Panel~B splits the sample at the global
  financial crisis; ``Short rate'' is the mean GDP-weighted G10 one-year yield
  and ``Dispersion'' the mean cross-country standard deviation of one-year
  yields in each regime. The 36-month rolling correlation of the two factors
  and the rolling short-rate level correlate $-0.57$.}
